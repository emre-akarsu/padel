Prop the phone 2 to 4 m away, side-on to you, so your whole body from head to feet is in the picture. You will go through the stroke one position at a time: move into the position shown, hold still, and you get that step's errors before the next one.

These checks are not coach-validated. Targets are the ideal avatar (Strike Player specs) seen from the side, with a tolerance band; the spec's coaching ranges widen the band where they exist. A 2D camera sees angles foreshortened when a limb points toward or away from the lens. Treat every pass/fail as a hint, not a verdict.

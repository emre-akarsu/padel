What is real and what is approximated

  • Real: stage order and timing come from RTMPose keypoints of a right-handed bandeja (PadelStar clip cLJVxtMlQRA, 1.8 to 4.0 s, 30 fps). Contact sits at 3.42 s, where the elbow straightens to about 174° in the 2D track.
  • Real: each spoken cue is grounded in the project's padel technique library (LTA overheads guide, Padelonomics photo sequence).
  • Approximated: the 3D poses are hand-set keyframes shaped to match the clip's 2D elbow, shoulder and knee angles. One camera in 2D cannot give true 3D, so treat the depth and rotations as an illustration.
  • Approximated: the player is a stylised mannequin dressed after your character image (green top, pleated skirt, blond ponytail, black racket). It is not a reconstruction of that person.
  • Voice: spoken by your phone's built-in voice, so it works offline and costs nothing; it sounds different on each device.
